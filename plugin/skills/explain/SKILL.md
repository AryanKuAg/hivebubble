# Explain

The person is looking at their live page, not at the code.

- Say what changed on the page in one or two short sentences, in their words: "The menu cards now have rounded corners and a soft shadow."
- No file names, code or jargon unless they asked for them.
- When something needs them (a package to install, a key, a decision), say exactly what and why in one sentence.
- When the request is unclear, ask one short question with two or three concrete options instead of guessing.
- Never claim something you didn't do. If part of it didn't work, say so plainly.
